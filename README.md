# Customer Personality Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyzes customer personality and purchasing behavior using Tableau. The dashboard helps businesses understand customer demographics, income levels, spending habits, and purchase trends to improve marketing strategies and customer engagement.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics.
- Compare average income across education levels.
- Study customer spending behavior.
- Analyze customer joining trends.
- Compare customer income with total spending.
- Create an interactive dashboard for business insights.

---

## Tools Used

- Tableau
- Microsoft Excel
- Data Cleaning

---

## Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards
  - Total Customers
  - Average Income
  - Average Total Spending
  - Average Recency

- Interactive Charts
  - Average Income by Education
  - Total Spending by Marital Status
  - Customer Joining Trend
  - Income Distribution
  - Income vs Total Spending (Scatter Plot)

- Interactive Filter
  - Education

---

## Key Insights

- Customers with higher income generally spend more.
- Spending behavior varies across marital status.
- Most customers belong to the middle-income group.
- Customer acquisition increased over the observed period.
- Education level influences average customer income.

---

## Business Benefits

- Helps identify high-value customers.
- Supports targeted marketing campaigns.
- Improves customer segmentation.
- Enables better business decision-making.
